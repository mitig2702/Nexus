# Nexus — Sample Questions (RAG-backed)

50 questions spanning all 40 ingested sources. Ask Nexus these directly (chat or `/rag/search`).

## HR & Policy
1. How many leaves am I entitled to per year, and do they carry over?
2. What's the process and documentation needed for sick leave over 3 days?
3. How many days of WFH can I take per week, and who approves it?
4. What's the maternity and paternity leave entitlement?
5. How much is the employee referral bonus, and when is it paid out?
6. When are performance reviews conducted, and what are the rating categories?

## IT Support
